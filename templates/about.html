About

A free public library of Islamic books

{{ app_name }} is an open digital library. Anyone may browse, search, and read every published book — free, without an account.

Our mission

Good books are the foundation of good learning. {{ app_name }} exists to make reliable Islamic literature — Qur'an studies, hadith, fiqh, history, education, and reference works — freely available to students, teachers, and curious readers everywhere, on any device.

What the library contains

The catalogue holds books in PDF form with full bibliographic information: title, author, description, category, language, and page count. Every book is read online in the browser or downloaded for offline reading. Search covers the full text inside every PDF, not just the titles.

How the library is managed

Books are added and maintained by library administrators. Visitors never need an account — reading is open to all. Changes to the catalogue are recorded in an internal audit log for accountability.

A note on the current collection: the catalogue currently contains short sample booklets produced for the launch of this platform. Real titles are welcome — see below.

Suggest a book or report a problem

If you know a book that belongs here, found a broken file, or have any other message for the library team, please use the contact form. Messages go directly to the administrators.
